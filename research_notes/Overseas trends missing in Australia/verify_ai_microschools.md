**Regulatory pathway: analyst background knowledge, NOT verified this session; confirm before use.**
- **Registered-school route.** Each state regulator registers or accredits non-government schools:
  - NSW: NESA, under the Education Act 1990.
  - VIC: VRQA, under the Education and Training Reform Act 2006 and its minimum standards.
  - QLD: NSSAB, under the Education (Accreditation of Non-State Schools) Act 2017, with provisional accreditation before opening.
  - Typical criteria: fit-and-proper governance, a curriculum aligned to state syllabuses or the Australian Curriculum, registered teachers, suitable premises, child-safe standards, and attendance records.
  - Lead times are often 12 months or more.
- **"Guides" instead of teachers.** States generally require teachers in registered schools to be registered or accredited with the state teacher regulator (e.g. NESA accreditation in NSW, VIT in Victoria, QCT in Queensland). This conflicts directly with Alpha's guide model. An Australian version would probably need registered teachers as teachers of record, with guides as extra staff.
- **For-profit status and funding.**
  - Commonwealth recurrent funding goes only to not-for-profit approved authorities.
  - NSW bars state funding to for-profit schools.
  - Some states may require registered schools to be not-for-profit (check VIC and QLD).
  - So a for-profit, tuition-only model may be legal in some states and not others.
- **Home-education hub route.** Children stay registered for home education with their state authority, and parents remain legally responsible. A full-time drop-off centre delivering the curriculum risks being treated as an unregistered school. Part-time enrichment hubs are lower-risk.
- **After-school and holiday programs.** These need no school registration. Child-safe standards and Working With Children Checks apply. This is the lowest-friction entry point.
- **AI policy context.** The Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools was endorsed by education ministers in 2023 (unverified). State tools such as NSWEduChat and SA EdChat are adjacent; only the 2023 SA trial is verified above.
