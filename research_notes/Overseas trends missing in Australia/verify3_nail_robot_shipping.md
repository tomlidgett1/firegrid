# Verify 3: Can Australians buy, or have they received, an automated home nail-painting (polish-applying) robot? (checked 13 Oct 2026)

**ANSWER: NO, nothing found (moderate confidence; absence can't be proven).** I found no polish-applying nail robot that is sold to, ships to, or has reached Australian consumers.
- **Nimble:** its own help centre says it ships to the **USA only**.
- **Aurami:** it is a pre-production prototype on Kickstarter. No units have been delivered anywhere, and I couldn't find which countries its campaign ships to.
- **Marketplaces:** Amazon AU, eBay AU, AliExpress and Temu turned up only nail-ART printers (excluded), hand-held drill/file "manicure robots", or nothing.

**What is still uncertain:**
- Nimble's 2021 Kickstarter took pledges from 25+ countries and promised "worldwide shipping". I couldn't confirm whether any Australian backer actually received a unit.
- Australians could also import a Nimble through a US freight forwarder. Nimble's returns policy mentions freight-forwarder customers.

**What this means for the claim:**
- "First/only robotic manicure station in Australia" looks supportable on this evidence.
- "Nothing similar exists in Australia" is riskier. A few Kickstarter or imported Nimble units could exist, and nail-art printers on Amazon AU are literally titled "Automatic Nail Painting Machine".

## 1. Does Nimble (nimblebeauty.com) ship to Australia, and did its Kickstarter rewards?

### Takeaway
Nimble's current official policy is to ship to the US only, with international shipping "hoped for" later. Its 2021 Kickstarter drew backers from 25+ countries and planned worldwide shipping, but I found no evidence that any units were delivered to Australia. I also found no Australian retailer, eBay AU listing or Australian review.

### Cited Findings
- **US-only shipping.** The help-centre article "Orders and Returns" says "Nimble currently ships to the United States of America only." It says international shipping is something the company hopes to add later, and that shipping is free on orders of US$50 or more. The article's date was not shown. [Nimble Help: Orders and Returns](https://help.nimblebeauty.com/en-US/articles/orders-and-returns-39903)
- **Terms of Use.** These say delivery "to some States and addresses outside the United States may be limited". This is boilerplate and does not name Australia. [Nimble Terms of Use](https://www.nimblebeauty.com/pages/terms-of-use)
- **Returns policy.** Customs and duties must be pre-paid when "returning from Canada or by way of a US military base or freight forwarder". This implies some units reach Canada or go out through forwarders. [Nimble Returns & Exchanges](https://www.nimblebeauty.com/pages/returns)
- **Warranty.** The warranty applies only in the country where the device was first sold. The search tool reported this page as about 1,442 days old, which is roughly late 2022. [Nimble Limited Product Warranty](https://www.nimblebeauty.com/pages/limited-product-warranty)
- **Kickstarter returns page.** Nimble has a separate "Kickstarter Returns & Exchanges Policy" page, which implies some Kickstarter units were delivered. [Nimble Kickstarter Returns Policy](https://www.nimblebeauty.com/pages/nimble-beauty-kickstarter-returns-exchanges-policy)
- **Pre-order store.** Nimble's BackerKit pre-order store is headed "FREE SHIPPING TO US ADDRESSES" (undated). [Nimble BackerKit preorders](https://nimble5e.backerkit.com/preorders)
- **Product site.** The device scans, paints and dries nails using AI. The site offers a 30-day money-back guarantee and a 1-year hardware warranty. The search extract showed no Australian availability or pricing. [nimblebeauty.com](https://www.nimblebeauty.com/)
- **Kickstarter campaign.**
  - Results: 5,877 backers pledged US$1,803,758 against a US$25,000 goal. The campaign ran 12 Apr to 28 May 2021. [Kickstarter: NIMBLE](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nimble/nimble-salon-quality-nails-from-the-comfort-of-your-home); [Kicktraq](https://www.kicktraq.com/projects/nimble/nimble-salon-quality-nails-from-the-comfort-of-your-home/)
  - The campaign FAQ was last updated 20 Mar 2025 and says backers get an email to pick capsule colours before shipping. It does not mention countries. [Kickstarter FAQ](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/nimble/nimble-salon-quality-nails-from-the-comfort-of-your-home//faqs)
- **International backers (strongest pro-"YES" signal).** An April 2021 press release reported more than 3,400 backers "from over 25 countries" about two weeks into the campaign. [Jotup press release](https://jotup.co/node/1306364)
- **Planned shipping.** Geeky Gadgets (Apr–May 2021) wrote that "worldwide shipping is expected ... around October 2021", with early-bird pledges from about US$249 / £180. [Geeky Gadgets 16 Apr 2021](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/nail-painting-machine-16-04-2021/); also [22 May](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/nail-painting-machine-22-05-2021/) and [29 May](https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/nail-painting-machine-29-05-2021/)
- **CES 2024.** Nimble was a CES 2024 Innovation Awards honoree as an "AI-powered manicure device for home use". [CES](https://www.ces.tech/ces-innovation-awards/2024/nimble-beauty-the-ai-powered-manicure-device-for-home-use/). Gizmodo's Best of CES 2024 found it "a little finicky" when reading the hand. [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/best-of-ces-2024-1851156359/27)
- **Price.** A syndicated consumer-tech article quoted US$599, a US$99 pre-order deposit and a March ship target. The search extract attributed it to CyberGuy, and its date is unclear. ["The biggest threat your nail salon has ever seen"](https://business.mammothtimes.com/mammothtimes/news/read/43869096/the_biggest_threat_your_nail_salon_has_ever_seen)
- **eBay AU.** An eBay AU-restricted search for "Nimble nail painting" returned only generic nail-art supplies: dotting tools, stampers, pens and press-ons. [eBay AU Nail Art Accessories](https://www.ebay.com.au/b/Nail-Art-Accessories/107876/bn_198308)
- **Nimble + Australia search.** This returned only Israeli and US launch coverage. [NoCamels 2021](https://nocamels.com/2021/05/nimble-robot-manicure-nails-startup); [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctech/articles/0,7340,L-3907398,00.html)
- **Do not confuse with other "Nimble" companies.**
  - `support.gonimble.com` ("Where does Nimble ship from") belongs to a different company, the Nimble phone-accessories brand. Ignore it. [gonimble](https://support.gonimble.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012587252-Where-does-Nimble-ship-from)
  - The Kickstarter "Nimble – the 1-finger wonder tool" is also a different product.

### Inferences
- Through official channels, a Nimble cannot be delivered to an Australian address. An Australian buyer would need a US freight forwarder and would lose warranty cover, since the warranty is limited to the country of sale.
- The 2021 Kickstarter is the only plausible route by which units reached Australia: there were 25+ backer countries and "worldwide shipping" was promised. However, Nimble's own store was still US-only into 2025–26, so international fulfilment may have been limited, refunded or not done. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- I could not read the Kickstarter reward "Ships to" field or the backer updates about international or Australian fulfilment. WebFetch was unavailable and the search snippets did not include them.
- I found no data on Australian backers and no Australian reviews or unboxings, though only one or two queries targeted this.
- The date of the help-centre article is unknown. Nothing contradicts the US-only policy, but I can't confirm it is current as of Oct 2026.
- I don't know whether the device is 110 V-only.
- I didn't search Amazon AU for "Nimble" by name. The Amazon AU search I did run showed no Nimble.

## 2. Does Aurami (or any other 2025–26 crowdfunded home nail robot) ship to Australia?

### Takeaway
Aurami is a polish-applying home nail robot on Kickstarter, but it is still pre-production. No consumer anywhere has a production unit, and I couldn't determine which countries it ships to, including Australia. No other polish-applying home robot crowdfunded in 2025–26 surfaced.

### Cited Findings
- **Kickstarter page.** "Aurami AI Nail Painting Device: Your Nail Salon at Home" advertises 15-minute painting, 0.1 mm precision and gentle UV curing. [Kickstarter: Aurami](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/aurami/aurami-ai-nail-painting-device-salon-nails-at-home)
- **Funding so far.** An undated Gizmocrowd review says the campaign had raised US$396,148 from 690 backers against a US$20,000 goal, with 38 days left. The current reward tier started at US$459. [Gizmocrowd](https://www.gizmocrowd.com/post/aurami-nail-machine-review)
- **Prototype status.** A Los Angeles event listing describes Aurami as "a functional prototype in the testing stage and preparing for mass production" and says the event is "not an official product launch".
  - Attendees were offered Kickstarter early access and a waitlist.
  - It quotes about 90 s per nail and about 15 min for both hands.
  - [Luma: Aurami Records](https://luma.com/gk76xr6h)
- **Press release.** A September 2026 press release says Aurami "announced the upcoming launch" of its AI-powered at-home manicure device, with about 30 s per nail for robotic application. It gives no price, ship date or territories. [KDH News press release](https://kdhnews.com/online_features/press_releases/aurami-brings-salon-quality-nails-home-with-an-ai-powered-15-minute-manicure-experience/article_935ff024-228c-5048-80c9-3b44e9efacd9.html)
- **Excluded look-alike.** Another Kickstarter project, "Naepca — Salon-quality Nails in 15S With 3D Printing", is a nail-art printer. [Kickstarter: Naepca](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/1816696999/jolinailportable-nail-kit-with-money-andtime-savings-for-you)

### Inferences
- No Australian has received an Aurami. At most, Australians might be able to pledge (pre-order) if the campaign ships internationally, which I couldn't verify. No delivery date has been published.

### Gaps
- I couldn't retrieve Aurami's "Ships to" list, estimated delivery date or campaign dates. The Gizmocrowd figures are undated.
- Other pre-launch home-robot startups did not surface in any search, and I didn't search for them by name because of the budget.

## 3. Are polish-applying nail robots listed on Amazon AU, eBay AU, AliExpress (shipping to AU), Temu AU, Kogan or Catch?

### Takeaway
No polish-applying robot was found on these sites.
- **Amazon AU:** the "automatic nail painting machine" results are all inkjet nail-ART printers (excluded).
- **eBay AU:** only manual nail-art supplies.
- **AliExpress:** the "robot / manicure robot / automatic nail painting" search pages hold printers and hand-held drill/file tools.
- **Temu:** nothing surfaced.
- **Kogan and Catch:** not searched.

### Cited Findings
- **Amazon AU — misleading title.** One listing is titled "3D Smart Nail Printer, Automatic Nail Painting Machine w/10.1 Inch Touch Screen, 2400DPI...", but it is a nail-art printer. [Amazon AU B0FND26J98](https://www.amazon.com.au/Printer-Automatic-Painting-Printing-Resolution/dp/B0FND26J98)
- **Amazon AU — other results, also printers.**
  - [B0BVHY9HHM](https://www.amazon.com.au/Automatic-Multifunction-Portable-Painting-Manicurist/dp/B0BVHY9HHM)
  - [B0BXBKM52B](https://www.amazon.com.au/Printer-Automatic-Machine-Portable-Painting/dp/B0BXBKM52B)

  - [YXCMD B0D1C8LYYR](https://www.amazon.com.au/YXCMD-Automatic-Identification-Printing-Possibilities/dp/B0D1C8LYYR)
  - [Amazon AU "nail printer" search](https://www.amazon.com.au/nail-printer/s?k=nail+printer)
- **eBay AU.** Results were stampers, dotting tools, pens and press-ons, with no robot. Example: [3D Nail Art Stamping](https://www.ebay.com.au/itm/406465377215)
- **AliExpress.** Search pages exist for "Robot Nail Machine", "Automatic nail polish machine", "Manicure robot" and "Automatic Nail Painting Machine". According to the search extract, they contain:
  - desktop nail printers, about US$1,500–1,900+, some salon-grade;
  - hand-held motorised file/drill "manicure robots", some with a built-in polish applicator.

  No autonomous polish-applying robot was identified, and shipping to Australia was not checked. [Robot Nail Machine](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-robot-nail-machine.html); [Automatic nail polish machine](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-automatic-nail-polish-machine.html); [Manicure robot](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-manicure-robot.html); [Automatic Nail Painting Machine](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-automatic-nail-painting-machine.html)
- **Accio (Alibaba B2B sourcing).** The "robot manicure painting machine" and "nail painting robot" pages show nail printers. One US$1,499 desktop printer is listed as compatible with AU/US/IT/ZA plugs; it is a printer, so excluded. [Accio: Robot Manicure Painting Machine](https://www.accio.com/plp/robot-manicure-painting-machine); [Accio: nail painting robot](https://www.accio.com/plp/nail-painting-robot)
- **Salon context (not consumer).** 10Beauty's robotic manicure system launched only in US salons, at one Ulta location. [NewBeauty](https://www.newbeauty.com/view/10beauty-robotic-manciure-system-launches-at-ulta-beauty); [Ulta beauty technology](https://www.ulta.com/beautyservices/beauty-technology)

### Inferences
- What Australians can readily buy is nail-ART printers, which is the excluded category, and sellers title them "Automatic Nail Painting Machine". Any "nothing similar" claim should therefore say "robot that applies real polish" or "full robotic manicure" so these listings don't contradict it.
- The AliExpress hand-held "manicure robots" are manual tools, not automated painting robots.

### Gaps
- I didn't search Kogan or Catch because of the budget.
- The Temu-restricted search ("nail painting robot machine") returned no Temu pages, only USPTO patents, so absence on Temu is only weakly established.
- I didn't verify AU shipping at the AliExpress listing level.
- A broad search, "robot nail painting device Australia buy", surfaced no Australian retailer or stockist of any nail robot.
