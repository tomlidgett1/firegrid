## Q1. Which climate/energy/circular business models scaled fastest overseas in 2024–2026?

### Takeaway
On hard numbers, two models scaled fastest:
- **Plug-in "balcony" solar.** Germany's register went from about 787k systems at end-2024 to about 1.0m by June 2025. The UK legalised it from 27 Aug 2026, and at least nine US states have passed laws.
- **Home-battery utilities that own the battery.** Base Power raised a US$1B Series C in Oct 2025.

A second tier has strong funding or early commercial proof but smaller absolute scale:
- compute waste-heat reuse (Deep Green, £200m in Jan 2024)
- industrial heat pumps sold as steam-as-a-service (Skyven, selected for up to US$145m from the US DOE)
- industrialised "serial" retrofit (Ecoworks, Energiesprong)
- cooling-as-a-service (Circolife)
- radiative-cooling coatings (i2Cool)

### Inferences
- Measured by unit adoption, plug-in solar is the stand-out category of 2024–26. Germany's register grew by roughly 24–30% in the first half of 2025, depending on which June snapshot is used. That compares 975,583 or 1,022,544 in June 2025 against 786,615 at end-2024.
- Measured by capital, Base Power stands out.
- The UK (Aug 2026) and US (2025–26) legislative waves suggest plug-in solar is moving from a German niche to a mainstream category across OECD markets.
- Several heavily funded models (Base Power, home-battery subscriptions, geothermal HVAC, EV battery certificates, radiative cooling, greywater) already have Australian equivalents. Fast overseas growth does not by itself mean an Australian gap.

## Q2. Which are absent from Australia despite strong fit? (Ranked candidates, best first, plus rejected ideas)

### Takeaway
Two models look clearly absent and fit Australia well:
- **Plug-in solar and plug-in battery kits.** Absent because they are illegal today, but a formal pathway is now being explored.
- **Compute waste-heat reuse at aquatic centres and hot-water systems.** Absent: the only Australian traces are a 2023 startup pitch and an ACT motion the government opposed in Sep 2026.

Two more appear absent as business models but got fewer Australia checks:
- industrial heat-pump steam-as-a-service
- prefabricated serial deep retrofit

Residential cooling subscriptions, urban-mining hubs and appliance repair-and-refurb networks are "Unclear". Several overseas winners already exist in Australia and are listed under "Rejected" with the Australian company named.

### Cited Findings

#### Candidate 1 — Plug-in ("balcony") solar and plug-in battery kits for renters, apartments and Solar Sharer households

**(1) Concept and revenue model**
- A kit of one to four panels and a microinverter, capped at around 800 W AC, plugs into an ordinary power point. Increasingly it comes with a plug-in battery.
- Kits are sold direct or through retail. UK kits are expected to cost about £400 and save £70–110 a year — [MoneyToTheMasses](https://moneytothemasses.com/news/plug-in-solar-panels-legalised-in-the-uk-costs-rules-and-savings-explained)
- Revenue would come from hardware margin, mounts and accessories, add-on batteries, and potentially app/energy-optimisation subscriptions.
- *Inference: an Australian version could be the first AS/NZS-certified kit, packaged with a strata/landlord approval workflow and an app that charges a plug-in battery during Solar Sharer free hours.*

**(2) Overseas exemplars and metrics**
- Germany:
  - Registered systems went from 786,615 (end-2024) to 1,022,544 (Jun 2025) — [energiezukunft](https://www.energiezukunft.eu/erneuerbare-energien/solarenergie/ueber-eine-million-balkonsolarkraftwerke-bereits-in-betrieb)
  - A lower dpa count of 975,583 on 10 Jun 2025 — [t-online/dpa](https://www.t-online.de/finanzen/aktuelles/id_100761870/eine-million-balkonkraftwerke.html)
  - About 135k added in Jan–Apr 2025, up 36% year on year — [ZfK](https://www.zfk.de/energie/strom/eine-million-balkonkraftwerke-am-netz-dunkelziffer-wohl-hoeher)
- UK: legal from 27 Aug 2026 (SI 2026/848; 800 W cap) — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/08/27/plug-in-solar-panels-now-legal-in-the-uk/)
- US:
  - Utah HB 340: signed Mar 2025, effective May 2025, up to 1,200 W — [Bright Saver](https://brightsaver.org/is-plug-in-solar-legal-/utah)
  - Nine states with laws, bills in at least 24 states — [Canary Media](https://www.canarymedia.com/articles/balcony-solar/clean-energy-plug-in-solar-panels)
- Coverage also points to a new wave of balcony products "with batteries included" — [RenewEconomy](https://reneweconomy.com.au/moves-afoot-to-bring-balcony-solar-to-australia-and-new-wave-of-products-has-batteries-included/)

**(3) Why it is accelerating now**
- Harmonised safety specs and caps:

- These standards answer the safety objections. Renters and apartment dwellers are otherwise locked out of rooftop solar.

### Inferences

## Q3. Which face Australian regulatory barriers, and are any barriers under review now?

### Takeaway
Plug-in solar is the only shortlisted model blocked outright by Australian rules. AS/NZS 4777.1 requires the inverter to be permanently connected on a dedicated circuit. That barrier is now under formal review:
- On 11 Sep 2026, energy ministers agreed to explore pathways.
- Minister Bowen points to Standards Australia work, state safety regulators and a review of state strata laws.
- Safety trials are planned with RMIT, UNSW and CSIRO (RACE for 2030).
- Advocates want legalisation by 1 Jul 2027. New Zealand is likely to move first.

Compute waste-heat reuse faces planning and land-use politics rather than legal bars. The other candidates face mainly commercial barriers.
