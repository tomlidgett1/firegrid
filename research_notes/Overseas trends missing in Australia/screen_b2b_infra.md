# Rapid screen: do 8 overseas-proven B2B, infrastructure, robotics and climate concepts already exist in Australia? (as at 13 Oct 2026)

## Key Question 1: For each concept, is there any Australian business, pilot or overseas entrant offering it or something closely similar?

### Takeaway
Three concepts already exist in Australia in a close form, so they are eliminated:
- **#1 shelf-scanning robots:** Badger Technologies robots ran in 16 Woolworths stores and checked shelf stock. Woolworths now trials shelf cameras.
- **#7 AI/automated check-in calls:** CareCob AI and CareCallingNow.
- **#8 success-fee valuation objections:** there is a NSW "no win, no fee" land-value objection service, plus fee-charging objection agents.

Three concepts are PARTIAL:
- **#4 autonomous airport wheelchairs:** WHILL's own Australian site already lists the service.
- **#5 air-con subscription:** no-upfront solar/battery subscriptions exist, but none includes air-con.
- **#6 serial prefab retrofit:** only conventional social-housing retrofit programs exist.

Only #3 (autonomous street sweepers) and #2 (drink-fridge restocking robots) showed no Australian trace at all.
