### Inferences
- Australian food-service buyers are now taking robots from overseas vendors (GMEX's order for at least 50 robots, JnS Robotics), so the appetite is there. But every robot found cooks or carries food, so the meal-tray assembly niche (ready-meal factories and airline, hospital and aged-care catering) still looks unserved.
- My Muscle Chef's own statement describes exactly the target customer: high volume, labour-intensive and lightly automated. Youfoodz, Lite n' Easy and the airline and hospital caterers are likely similar, but this pass did not verify that.
- Chef Robotics bundles its robots with tray-sealing equipment from Proseal (JBT Marel) and Packline, so those partners could one day carry it into Australia. This is speculative: no Australian rollout was found.

### Gaps

The combined trade-mark query also returned no Dexai or Eatch records, but a search extract cannot prove a negative.
- Hyphen was not searched by name. Australian catering kitchens (dnata, gategroup) and HealthShare NSW's central production kitchens were not searched one by one.
- A trial under a non-disclosure agreement would not show up in web search. The app-store angle does not fit a business-to-business robot, so it was not used.
