# Rapid screen: 8 overseas-proven consumer concepts vs Australia

Screen run 13 Oct 2026. 47 WebSearch calls in "standard" mode, against a hard cap of 48.

WebFetch was unavailable, so every finding comes from search-result extracts. Several Australian articles had no date in the extract. Company numbers are self-reported.

Classification key:
- **LIKELY ABSENT:** nothing similar found.
- **PARTIAL:** adjacent or partial substitutes found.
- **PRESENT:** an Australian business, or an overseas company serving Australians, already does it.

## Key question 1: For each concept, is there any Australian business, pilot, or overseas entrant offering it or something closely similar?

### Takeaway
Only two concepts turned up no Australian equivalent after targeted searches meant to find one:
- **#2:** a creator-led 7-a-side football league.
- **#3:** a vaulted graded-card marketplace with instant buyback and digital pack rips.

The other six:
- **#1 (Breeze-style date booking):** has a close but small Australian analogue, Humpday.
- **#4 to #8:** already exist in Australia, via Reebelo, Rntr/Hello Tailr, Sapyen, Smartsalary/E-stralian/ANU, and Borne/Park Hyatt respectively.
