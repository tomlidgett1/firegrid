# Verification: Residential reverse-cycle air-con / heat-pump subscription for landlords and households (Australia presence check)

Independent adversarial verification, as at 13 October 2026. Method: 38 WebSearch calls (standard mode). 32 were presence checks (84%) and 6 were fact or overseas-growth checks. WebFetch was unavailable, so all evidence comes from search-result extracts. Details attributed to a page come from those extracts, not from a full read of the page. No other files in research_notes were read.

---

## 1. Verdict: does this business (or anything closely similar) already exist in Australia?

### Takeaway
**VERDICT: PRESENT. Confidence: 75%.** Mr Rental is a national Australian rent-to-own chain. It rents brand-new wall-mounted (split-system) air conditioners with delivery and full installation included, from $32 a week on a 36-month term, backed by a repair-or-replace guarantee. The brief's own rules count this as "same or similar" ("rent-to-own appliance businesses offering split-system air-conditioners with installation/repairs (e.g., Mr Rental …)"). However, I found nothing in Australia that matches the specific pitch: a subscription aimed at landlords that delivers minimum-standards compliance and includes servicing, end-of-life replacement, smart monitoring and optional heat-pump hot water. A new entrant could stand out on that segment, but could not claim to be first to put air-con on a monthly rental with installation included.

### Cited Findings
- Mr Rental rents "Wall Mounted Air Conditioner – Large" units: "From $32.00* per week. Brand new. 36 month rental." Full installation is included in the price, next-business-day delivery is offered, and other terms are available on request. — [Mr Rental – Large wall-mounted AC](https://www.mrrental.com.au/appliances/air-conditioners/large-wall-mounted-air-conditioner)
- Mr Rental's medium wall-mounted units are "delivered, assembled & fully installed". Installation is included in the rental, and payments can be weekly, fortnightly or monthly. — [Mr Rental – Medium wall-mounted AC](https://www.mrrental.com.au/appliances/air-conditioners/medium-wall-mounted-air-conditioner)
- A Mr Rental blog post (about 513 days old at search time, so roughly May 2025) says "Mr Rental installs both wall-mounted and split system air conditioning units." — [Mr Rental blog – Guide to appliance rentals](https://www.mrrental.com.au/blog/your-guide-to-appliance-rentals-and-hiring-appliances)
- Mr Rental's own staff ("Mobile Representatives") deliver and install all products in the home. — [Mr Rental FAQs](https://www.mrrental.com.au/why-rent/faqs)
- Mr Rental says it will repair a faulty product immediately or replace it. A "happiness guarantee" covers product swaps, repairs or replacements and "anytime upgrades", and the "MrFixed" plan includes repair if the product breaks down. The extract did not say which of these pages each statement came from. — [Mr Rental – Why rent](https://www.mrrental.com.au/why-rent/renting-from-mr-rental); [Mr Rental – Rental options](https://www.mrrental.com.au/your-options/rental-options)
- Mr Rental also has a business rental arm (the Business page and "MrCorp", with weekly payments). — [Mr Rental – Business](https://www.mrrental.com.au/business); [Appliance Retailer](https://www.applianceretailer.com.au/?p=46861)
- No Australian landlord-compliance air-con or heat-pump subscription was found. Searches for "no upfront cost" heating offers to Victorian landlords, property-manager air-con packages, and rental-upgrade packages returned only government guidance and rebate information. — [energy.vic.gov.au – Energy efficiency for rental properties](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/renewable-energy/victorias-gas-substitution-roadmap/energy-efficiency-for-rental-properties-in-victoria); [Raine & Horne Sunbury – Rental minimum standards](https://legacy.raineandhorne.com.au/sunbury/rental-minimum-standards-victoria)
- Australia's no-upfront home-energy subscriptions (Zelora, Enreal, EnergyAustralia Solar Plus) cover solar and batteries only. No air-con or heat-pump product was found. — [SolarQuotes – Zelora](https://www.solarquotes.com.au/blog/bunnings-zelora-solar-battery-mb3232/); [Smart – Enreal offer](https://www.smart.com.au/p/enrealoffer/); [SolarQuotes – EnergyAustralia Solar Plus](https://www.solarquotes.com.au/blog/energy-australia-solar-plus/)

### Inferences
- **Why PRESENT rather than PARTIAL.** Mr Rental is an ongoing, national, commercial service. For a household, it does essentially what the concept does: no capital outlay, a periodic fee, a new split system supplied and installed, and repairs or replacement during the term. The brief explicitly names this type of operator as counting. A wall-mounted unit installed by a technician is in practice a split system, and Mr Rental's own blog says so.
- **Why only 75%.** (a) No extract confirms that Mr Rental's units are reverse-cycle; cooling-only splits are sold in Queensland and the NT, where Mr Rental started. (b) Periodic servicing is promised for fridges but was not found for air conditioners; only repair or replace was found. (c) It is a consumer lease aimed at renters and credit-constrained households, not a landlord or compliance product. A strict reviewer focused on landlords could call this PARTIAL.
- **The landlord job looks unserved.** Victoria's minimum standards fall on the rental provider. An air-con a tenant rents from Mr Rental does not obviously make the landlord compliant, and a fixed split installation needs the landlord's permission anyway. This is my reading of the rules; I did not find a legal source on it. On the evidence found, the B2B "compliance-as-a-service for rental providers" segment is open.
- **Fast-follower risk is real.** Bunnings and Intellihub (Zelora), Intellihub (Enreal) and EnergyAustralia already run no-upfront subscriptions with installation, monitoring and maintenance. LG is reported to be exploring a direct-to-consumer subscription model in Australia (see section 3).

### Gaps
- Not confirmed: whether Mr Rental's wall-mounted units are reverse-cycle (heat and cool), whether air-con rentals include periodic servicing, the price of the medium unit, whether installation is available in every franchise region, and whether a landlord can rent units through Mr Rental Business or MrCorp for a tenanted property.
- Full product pages and terms could not be fetched. The MrFlex terms state "1 month rental payment is required upfront" ([Mr Rental MrFlex terms](https://mrrental.com.au/terms-mrflex)), so "no upfront cost" may in practice mean about one month in advance. It is not confirmed whether this applies to air-con rentals.

## 2. Can an Australian landlord or household get reverse-cycle air-con or a heat pump on subscription/rental with installation and maintenance included today?

### Takeaway
**Households: yes, broadly.** Mr Rental rents new wall-mounted or split air-cons with installation included and a repair-or-replace guarantee, from $32 a week over 36 months. Whether the units are reverse-cycle and whether servicing is included are unconfirmed. **Landlords: no dedicated offer found.** The routes open to them are buying outright with Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) or Energy Savings Scheme (ESS) discounts, government interest-free loans, or financing. **Heat pumps:** I found no Australian heat-pump hot-water or hydronic heat-pump rental or subscription. Only financing (Solahart with Zip) appeared.

### Cited Findings
- **Mr Rental (household rental):** see section 1. Large unit from $32 a week, brand new, 36-month term, installation included. Medium unit installed, payable weekly, fortnightly or monthly. — [Mr Rental Large](https://www.mrrental.com.au/appliances/air-conditioners/large-wall-mounted-air-conditioner); [Mr Rental Medium](https://www.mrrental.com.au/appliances/air-conditioners/medium-wall-mounted-air-conditioner)
- **Servicing:** Mr Rental's fridge page says every fridge rental includes "full servicing and maintenance support". No equivalent air-con statement was found. Business rentals exclude repairs for damage. — [Mr Rental Fridges](https://www.mrrental.com.au/appliances/fridges); [Mr Rental Business](https://www.mrrental.com.au/business)
- **Generic searches turned up no other provider.** "Air conditioner subscription Australia no upfront" returned only overseas subscriptions (LG Subscribe Malaysia, Droxcare India) and Australian buy-now-pay-later or finance offers. — [says.com – LG Subscribe Malaysia aircon](https://says.com/my/lifestyle/lg-subscribe-heatwave-aircon); [Bing Lee – Afterpay air conditioners](https://www.binglee.com.au/articles/help/ways-to-pay/afterpay/air-conditioners); [Samsung AU – Glow finance listing](https://www.samsung.com/au/air-conditioners/ducted/ac200jxafnh-sa)
- **Landlord route (Victoria): rebates and loans, not subscriptions.** "Every single upgrade a property may need under the new minimum efficiency standards is eligible for a discount under our Victorian Energy Upgrades program". Eligible rental providers can also apply for an interest-free loan; the amount was truncated in the extract. — [energy.vic.gov.au – rental properties](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/renewable-energy/victorias-gas-substitution-roadmap/energy-efficiency-for-rental-properties-in-victoria); [energy.vic.gov.au – rental-provider guidance (docx)](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/word_doc/0025/775321/energy-efficiency-for-rental-homes-in-victoria-information-for-rental-providers-accessible.docx)
- **Heat-pump hot water: financing, not rental.** Solahart's Victorian offer runs through Zip, from $14.95 a week, with a $99 Zip establishment fee, until 31 August 2026. — [Solahart – Victorian Zip heat pump offer](https://solahart.com.au/victorian-zip-heat-pump-offer)
- **Residential heating- or cooling-as-a-service:** none found. The only Australian "CHAAS" offer (SmartConsult) charges for heating and cooling produced by commercial energy centres. — [HVACR News – cooling as a service](https://hvacrnews.com.au/tag/cooling-as-a-service/)

### Inferences
- A household (owner-occupier or renter with landlord consent) can already avoid buying an air-con outright through Mr Rental. A landlord seeking a compliant, serviced, replace-at-end-of-life solution has no subscription option found. They must pay capex, offset by VEU discounts and loans, or use finance.
- VEU discounts and interest-free loans cut the landlord's capex barrier. This weakens the "no capital outlay" value proposition, and a subscription would need to capture the VEU certificate value itself.

### Gaps
- I could not check the full Mr Rental product pages for brand, kW, reverse-cycle status or servicing terms.
- Victorian installers advertising "$0 upfront" VEU-funded split systems to landlords were not searched individually. These would in any case be one-off discounted sales (adjacent, not the same).

## 4. What Australian facts matter for launch?

### Takeaway
Victoria now has binding, dated triggers. From **1 March 2027**, heating and hot water in rentals that reach end of life must be replaced with efficient electric units, and new leases need efficient cooling. From **1 July 2030**, every rental needs efficient cooling. A single reverse-cycle air-con satisfies both the heating and cooling requirements. About 28% of Victorian households rent. Household rentals would fall under consumer-lease cost caps; landlord (investment) contracts likely would not, though this is my inference. NSW is consulting on similar standards.

### Cited Findings

**Top five demand and regulatory facts**
1. **Victoria, 1 March 2027.** New minimum energy-efficiency standards for rentals phase in from this date. Heating and hot-water systems that reach end of life must be replaced with efficient electric systems (heat-pump hot water or reverse-cycle air-con), and main living areas must have efficient cooling at the start of a new lease. — [energy.vic.gov.au](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/renewable-energy/victorias-gas-substitution-roadmap/energy-efficiency-for-rental-properties-in-victoria); [Consumer Affairs Victoria – new minimum energy efficiency standards](https://www.consumer.vic.gov.au/resources-and-tools/legislation/public-consultations-and-reviews/new-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards); [Tenants Victoria, June 2025 announcement](https://tenantsvic.org.au/news/we-welcome-new-air-conditioning-measures/)
2. **Victoria, 1 July 2030.** All rental homes must have efficient cooling in the main living area, regardless of when the lease began, unless an exemption applies. "Installing a reverse-cycle air conditioner will acquit both the heating and cooling requirements." Every required upgrade is eligible for a VEU discount. — [energy.vic.gov.au – rental-provider guidance](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/__data/assets/word_doc/0025/775321/energy-efficiency-for-rental-homes-in-victoria-information-for-rental-providers-accessible.docx); [energy.vic.gov.au](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/renewable-energy/victorias-gas-substitution-roadmap/energy-efficiency-for-rental-properties-in-victoria)
3. **Victorian rental households.** 28% of Victorian households were renting at the 2021 Census. Renting is concentrated in inner Melbourne: 64% in the City of Melbourne and 47% in Port Phillip. — [Renting in Victoria Snapshot 2024 (vic.gov.au)](https://www.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-04/Renting-in-Victoria-Snapshot-2024.pdf)
4. **Consumer-lease rules (National Consumer Credit Protection Act, NCCP).** Since the Financial Sector Reform Act 2022 (December 2022), a consumer lease's total cost is capped at the base price plus 4% of the base price per month of term (maximum 48 months), plus only permitted fees (currently a reasonable delivery fee). A "protected earnings" limit caps repayments at 10% of after-tax income. ASIC has taken enforcement action since, and some providers have switched to sale-by-instalment credit, which carries a 48% annual cost-rate cap. — [ASIC – consumer lease industry on notice](https://asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/news-items/consumer-lease-industry-on-notice-for-potential-compliance-failures-following-reforms/); [Brightlaw – draft bill](https://www.brightlaw.com.au/small-amount-credit-contract-consumer-lease-draft-bill/); [APH – ASIC submission](https://aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Corporations_and_Financial_Services/OversightofASIC/-/media/F8C1F7F0313648ECB419881907F895D4.ashx)
5. **Other states.** NSW is considering minimum energy-efficiency rental standards (MEERS) that could include ceiling insulation, efficient hot water or reverse-cycle air-cons. Consultation closed on 31 May 2026. In September 2026, Energy Consumers Australia urged NSW to proceed, estimating renter savings of $300 to $570 a year. The ACT already requires minimum ceiling insulation in rentals and consulted in 2024 on heating and cooling requirements; adoption is unconfirmed. — [Energy Consumers Australia, 9 September 2026](https://energyconsumersaustralia.com.au/news/nsw-government-urged-introduce-minimum-energy-efficiency-standards-save-renters-570-year); [NSW MEERS](https://www.energy.nsw.gov.au/meers); [ACT Planning – energy efficiency](https://www.planning.act.gov.au/professionals/regulation-and-responsibilities/responsibilities/energy-efficiency)

**Other supporting facts**
- Victoria's existing rule, in force since 29 March 2021, requires a fixed heater in the main living area. A non-ducted air-con or heat pump must have a 2-star or better energy rating. — [Consumer Affairs Victoria – heating standard (docx)](https://cms9.consumer.vic.gov.au/library/publications/housing-and-accommodation/renting/minimum-rental-standards-for-heating--residential-tenancies-regulations-2021.docx)
- Industry summaries describe the cooling minimum as a 3-star fixed cooling appliance, or a 2-star equivalent central cooler. — [Raine & Horne Sunbury](https://legacy.raineandhorne.com.au/sunbury/rental-minimum-standards-victoria); [HVACR News](https://hvacrnews.com.au/news/air-conditioning-mandatory-in-new-rental-standards/)
- From 25 November 2025, it is an offence to advertise or offer a Victorian rental that does not meet minimum standards. — [Tenants Victoria – minimum standards](https://tenantsvic.org.au/advice/new-rental-laws/minimum-standards/)
- The 2024 consultation estimated the average extra cost of all proposed requirements at A$5,519 per property. That proposal had an earlier start date of 30 October 2025, which was later replaced by the phase-in from 1 March 2027. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/victoria-is-raising-minimum-rental-standards-its-good-news-for-tenants-and-the-environment-231679); [PropertyUpdate](https://propertyupdate.com.au/victoria-proposes-new-rental-laws-heres-what-it-could-mean-for-investors/)
- Exemptions include rentals served by centralised building systems and cases of insufficient space or excessive cost. — [energy.vic.gov.au](https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/renewable-energy/victorias-gas-substitution-roadmap/energy-efficiency-for-rental-properties-in-victoria)
- Tenants Victoria's 2026 electorate profiles, based on the 2021 Census, show 122.8K renting households in Southern Metropolitan (31%) and 75.3K in Western Victoria (21%). — [Tenants Victoria – Southern Metropolitan](https://tenantsvic.org.au/wp-content/uploads/electorates/southern-metropolitan.pdf); [Tenants Victoria – Western Victoria](https://tenantsvic.org.au/wp-content/uploads/electorates/western-victoria.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Demand timing.** Cooling for new leases from 1 March 2027 creates demand more than three years before the 2030 deadline for all rentals. Rental turnover will drive installations from 2027, and the 2030 cut-off creates a backlog peak.
- **Regulatory structure** (my inference; needs legal advice):
  - A subscription sold to a household for personal or household use, with charges above the base price and no purchase right, would probably be a consumer lease. That means the 4% per month cap, responsible-lending obligations and an Australian Credit Licence.
  - A contract with a landlord hiring equipment for an investment property is probably for business or investment purposes and outside the NCCP consumer-lease regime.
  - A landlord-first (B2B) model is therefore simpler to run and avoids the reputational baggage of rent-to-own.
- **VEU is double-edged.** It subsidises the equipment, which improves subscription unit economics if the provider captures the certificates. But it also lowers landlords' upfront cost, weakening the case for "no capital outlay".

### Gaps
- No absolute count of Victorian rental households for 2026 was found; only the 28% share and some electorate counts. Roughly 0.7 million would be my estimate, not a sourced figure.
- The amount of the Victorian interest-free loan for rental providers was truncated in the extract ("up to $1,400…"). It needs checking.
- Dates conflict across sources. The renter guide gives 1 July 2027 for draught-proofing, while a June 2025 Premier's release gives different starts for some items ([Premier's release PDF](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-06/250624-Securing-Victoria's-Gas-Supply-While-Slashing-Energy-Bills.pdf)). The 1 March 2027 and 1 July 2030 heating and cooling dates appear consistently.
- Not researched within the search budget: national split-system unit sales, current electricity prices, and the exact NCCP definition and exclusions for consumer leases (personal or household purpose test).
